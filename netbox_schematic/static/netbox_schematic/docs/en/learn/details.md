# Details

The panel on the right is the "passport" of whatever you clicked. It goes deeper than a tooltip: a tooltip answers "what is this", the details answer "what can I do with it".

## Device

Click the **name** on a node (or in the tree, or in the rack view): model, role, location, serial number, all ports with their occupancy, IP addresses, stack. From here — editing fields (the pencil), assigning an IP, «+ порт» (add port). More detail — the "Device specs" topic.

## Power panel

Click the panel name: the list of feeds with their parameters (volts/amps/phases), renaming, «+ фидер» (add feed). Clicking a feed highlights its path.

## How the panel behaves

- Opening the details closes any lingering tooltips — the screen does not get cluttered.
- While you edit fields the scroll position is kept; closing the panel resets it.
- To close: the cross, or a click on the schema background.

## On a phone

The details slide up from the bottom and take almost the whole screen. Port tooltips appear next to the port; action blocks (picking a model, shifting port numbers) are centered on the screen.
